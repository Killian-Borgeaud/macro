Figure. The Golden Rule. Steady-state consumption per worker c*(s) as a function of the saving rate s. Hill-shaped: too little saving and you produce too little; too much saving and you never consume. The maximum sits at s** = α (marked). At low s, ↑s helps consumption; past the peak, the trade-off reverses.
