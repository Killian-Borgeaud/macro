# Module 2 — Open-Economy Solow and Technological Progress

Maps to **Lecture 2** in the course plan. Covers textbook **Chapters 4 and 5**.

## Files in this module

- `Lecture 2a.md` — Chapter 4: the Solow model for a small open economy. Capital mobility, current account = savings minus investment, convergence under capital mobility.
- `Lecture 2b.md` — Chapter 5: the general Solow model with exogenous technological progress. Total factor productivity B grows at a constant rate; balanced growth in steady state with positive growth in GDP per worker.
- `Slides_Macro3_Session2.md` — Exercise session by Maria Bolboaca: small open economy (Exercise 2.1) and general Solow (Exercise 2.2). Note the unusual filename (`Macro3_Session2` instead of `Session2`).

For visually-heavy pages (diagrams of capital flows, steady-state phase plots), use the corresponding `.pages/p{NNN}.png` files.

## Textbook chapters

- `textbook_ch4.md` — *Wealth accumulation and capital mobility: the Solow model for a small open economy*.
- `textbook_ch5.md` — *Technological progress and growth: the general Solow model*. Introduces the time-varying B_t that makes long-run per-worker growth possible.

## Connections to other modules

- **← Module 1**: directly extends the basic Solow model from Lecture 1b/1c. The closed-economy setup is assumed background.
- **→ Module 3**: Chapter 5's exogenous technological progress is the gap that human capital (Ch 6) and scarce resources (Ch 7) attempt to plug — both still treat A_t as exogenous but address steady-state predictions about levels.
- **→ Modules 4, 5**: endogenizing the growth of A_t (instead of assuming it exogenous) is the entire point of the endogenous-growth literature in later modules.
