# Module 3 — Human Capital and Scarce Resources

Maps to **Lecture 3** in the course plan. Covers textbook **Chapters 6 and 7**.

## Files in this module

- `Lecture 3a.md` — Chapter 6: the Solow model with human capital (Mankiw-Romer-Weil 1992). Adds h_t (human capital per worker) to the production function; addresses two empirical problems of the basic Solow model (rate of convergence too fast; investment-rate effect on per-capita GDP too small).
- `Lecture 3b.md` — Chapter 7: limits to growth? The Solow model with scarce natural resources (land, oil). Classical Smith/Ricardo/Malthus pessimism revisited; whether technological progress can outpace resource scarcity.
- `Slides_Session3.md` — Exercise session by Maria Bolboaca: Solow with human capital (Exercise 3.1) and Solow with oil (Exercise 3.2).

For visually-heavy pages (production-function diagrams, phase plots), use the corresponding `.pages/p{NNN}.png` files.

## Textbook chapters

- `textbook_ch6.md` — *Education and growth: the Solow model with human capital*. The MRW extension.
- `textbook_ch7.md` — *Limits to growth? The Solow model with scarce natural resources*. Solow model augmented with a fixed resource input.

## Connections to other modules

- **← Module 2**: both Ch 6 and Ch 7 are extensions of the general Solow framework (Ch 5). Technological progress is still treated as exogenous here.
- **→ Module 4**: the unresolved question — *what determines technological progress?* — motivates the move to endogenous growth (productive externalities in Ch 8, R&D in Ch 9).
- **Cross-link**: Ch 6 introduces the MRW empirical estimation methodology, which is referenced when evaluating later models.
