## CONCLUSIONS BASED ON THE BASIC SOLOW MODEL

- What can a (poor) country do to create a transitory growth in GDP per worker resulting in a permanently higher level of income and consumption per worker? The basic Solow model provides the following answers:
- Increase the savings rate
- Reduce the growth rate of the labour force
- Reduce the rate of depreciation, i.e., invest better
- Improve the level of technology
