# Module 1 — Stylized Facts and the Basic Solow Model

Maps to **Lecture 1** in the course plan. Covers textbook **Chapters 1, 2, 3**.

## Files in this module

- `Lecture 1a.md` — Stylized facts about prosperity and growth: GDP per worker, the Lorenz curve, Gini coefficient, absolute vs conditional convergence, balanced growth. Based on Chapter 2.
- `Lecture 1b.md` — The basic (closed-economy) Solow model: agents, markets, capital accumulation. Conceptual setup, based on Chapter 3.
- `Lecture 1c.md` — Continuation of 1b: the complete Solow model with all equations (Cobb-Douglas, factor prices, savings, capital accumulation, labour growth), plus government sector.
- `Slides_Session1.md` — Exercise session by Maria Bolboaca: recap of Lecture 1 topics, discussion of take-home exercises.

For visually-heavy pages (diagrams, Lorenz curves, GDP plots), use the corresponding `.pages/p{NNN}.png` files.

## Textbook chapters

- `textbook_ch1.md` — *Macroeconomics for the long run and for the short run*: framing chapter, distinguishes long-run from short-run questions.
- `textbook_ch2.md` — *Some facts about prosperity and growth*: the empirical motivation behind the whole course (stylized facts 1-7).
- `textbook_ch3.md` — *Capital accumulation and growth: the basic Solow model*: the canonical derivation that everything later extends.

## Connections to other modules

- **→ Module 2**: extends the basic Solow model to (a) a small open economy with capital mobility (Ch 4) and (b) exogenous technological progress (Ch 5). Module 2 assumes you've mastered the closed-economy steady-state mechanics from Ch 3 / Lecture 1b-c.
- **→ Module 3 and beyond**: every later growth model (human capital, scarce resources, endogenous growth) builds on the Cobb-Douglas + capital accumulation + balanced growth scaffolding introduced here.
