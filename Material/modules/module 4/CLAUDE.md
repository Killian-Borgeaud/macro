# Module 4 — Endogenous Growth: Externalities and R&D

Maps to **Lecture 4** in the course plan. Covers textbook **Chapters 8 and 9**.

## Files in this module

- `Lecture 4a.md` — Chapter 8: productive externalities and endogenous growth. Two flavours of endogenous growth introduced (externality-based vs R&D-based); externality version assumes a firm's productivity depends on aggregate capital, generating increasing returns at the aggregate level.
- `Lecture 4b.md` — Chapter 9: R&D-based endogenous growth. Technological progress as the output of a labour-intensive R&D production process; ideas as the direct output; macroeconomic modelling of the R&D sector.
- `Slides_Session4.md` — Exercise session by Maria Bolboaca: endogenous growth with productive externalities (Exercise 4.1) and with R&D (Exercise 4.2).

For visually-heavy pages, use the corresponding `.pages/p{NNN}.png` files.

## Textbook chapters

- `textbook_ch8.md` — *Productive externalities and endogenous growth*. The externality-based endogenization.
- `textbook_ch9.md` — *R&D-based endogenous growth*. Romer-style R&D production process; baseline R&D-driven growth model.

## Connections to other modules

- **← Modules 2, 3**: both prior modules treated technological progress as exogenous (Ch 5, 6, 7). This module endogenizes it.
- **→ Module 5**: the R&D model in Ch 9 has a **scale effect** (more population → faster growth) that empirically fails. Module 5 (Lectures 5a/5b) introduces the "no scale effects" refinements (Jones 1995, Kortum, Segerstrom) and the truly-endogenous variants (Smulders-Van de Klundert, Howitt, Peretto, Young).
- **Notational continuity**: A_t (technology level) and the R&D labour share L_A / L_Y carry directly into Module 5.
